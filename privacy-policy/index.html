Privacy Policy for LouveesUploads

Last updated: May 21, 2026  |  Effective date: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LouveesUploads ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our desktop application to upload videos to TikTok. We are committed to protecting your privacy. LouveesUploads is designed to collect the absolute minimum data necessary to function.

1. Who We Are

LouveesUploads is a personal desktop tool developed for individual use. It is not a commercial service, and it does not operate any servers, databases, or cloud infrastructure that stores user data. Contact: louveesuploader@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

LouveesUploads collects only what is strictly necessary to authenticate with TikTok and upload your videos. The table below summarises exactly what data is involved:

Data TypePurposeWhere StoredShared?
TikTok OAuth access tokenAuthenticates API requests to upload videos on your behalfLocally on your device only (tiktok_token.json)No
TikTok refresh tokenRenews the access token when it expires without requiring re-loginLocally on your device onlyNo
TikTok user ID (open_id)Returned by TikTok's API to confirm authentication succeededNot persistently storedNo
Video files you choose to uploadTransmitted directly to TikTok's servers via their APINot stored by the App — only on your device and TikTok's serversOnly with TikTok

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, IP address, location, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other personal data.

3. How We Use Your Information

The TikTok OAuth token is used for one purpose only: to make authenticated API calls to TikTok's Content Posting API to upload videos you have selected. It is never used for any other purpose, and it is never transmitted to any server other than TikTok's own API endpoints.

4. Data Storage and Security

All authentication tokens are stored in a local file (tiktok_token.json) on your own computer. LouveesUploads does not operate any remote servers or cloud storage. The security of this file depends on the security of your own device. We recommend:

  • Keeping your operating system and security software up to date
  • Not sharing your device or the token file with others
  • Revoking the App's TikTok access via TikTok's Security Settings if you no longer use the App

5. Data Retention

Authentication tokens are stored on your device until you manually delete the tiktok_token.json file or revoke access through TikTok's settings. LouveesUploads does not retain any data on external systems, so there is no remote data to delete.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

LouveesUploads does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your data with any third parties, advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. The only data transmission that occurs is between your device and TikTok's official API servers when you choose to upload a video. This transmission is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

7. TikTok API and Third-Party Services

LouveesUploads uses the TikTok for Developers API. When you authenticate and upload content, your data is also subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy, available at https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy. We are not responsible for TikTok's data practices.

8. Children's Privacy

LouveesUploads is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used this App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Since LouveesUploads stores no personal data on any servers, the only data subject to these rights is the local token file on your own device, which you can delete at any time. To revoke TikTok API access, go to your TikTok account settings under Security & Permissions and remove LouveesUploads from connected apps.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of LouveesUploads after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: louveesuploader@gmail.com
