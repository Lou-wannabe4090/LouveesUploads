Privacy Policy for LouveesUploader

Last updated: May 2026

Information We Collect

LouveesUploader collects only the TikTok OAuth access token required to upload videos on your behalf. We do not collect your name, email, or any other personal information.

How We Use Your Information

Your TikTok access token is used solely to authenticate with the TikTok API and publish videos you provide to your TikTok profile.

Data Storage

Your access token is stored locally on your own device. LouveesUploader does not transmit or store your data on any external servers.

Data Sharing

LouveesUploader does not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties.

Third Party Services

LouveesUploader uses the TikTok API. Please refer to TikTok's Privacy Policy for information on how TikTok handles your data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: louveesuploader@gmail.com
